Question

Only ready documents can be selected.
Up to 2,000 characters. Search shows matches; Answer may also ask Gemini.

Limits and data use

Use public or fictional documents only. Each file can be up to 1 MiB; at most 10 documents can be active. PDFs must have text on every page, with at most 50 pages and 200,000 extracted characters. Scans and encrypted PDFs are unsupported. The first upload or search may take longer while the local embedding model loads.

Upload sends document text, metadata and vectors to Pinecone. Search sends a question vector to Pinecone. Answer also sends the question and selected fragments to Gemini. These actions may use paid services. Similarity scores describe vector similarity, not answer correctness. Check important claims against cited text.
